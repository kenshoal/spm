## Scope

- **Included:** AC1 to AC12 at the unit, API, repository and database layers; the D6 change to the three existing capacity checks; and the inbox lines for the six notice types.
- **Excluded:** the Attendee waitlist page (no Attendee event frontend yet, per SCRUM-21 increment 2); email channels (SCRUM-57); a capacity increase opening places (no story raises capacity yet).

## Actors and permissions

- **Attendee** (`attendee` role): joins, lists, accepts and withdraws **their own** entries only.
- **Coordinator:** the latest `event_coordinator_assignments` row for the event may choose a recipient (D9; the role is still open in ledger §D).
- Every other role gets 403, no session gets 401, and the browser roles cannot read the tables or execute the functions.

## Allowed state transitions

| From | To | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| — | `Waiting` | AC1 join |
| `Waiting` | `Offered` | AC2 FIFO offer, AC3 Coordinator choice, AC9 hand-over |
| `Offered` | `Accepted` | AC4 accept (creates a `Registered` registration) |
| `Offered` | `Waiting` (back, new `queued_at`) | AC4 expiry (D3) |
| `Offered` | `Waiting` (same `queued_at`) | AC3 revoked by a Coordinator choice (D9) |
| `Waiting` / `Offered` | `Withdrawn` | AC6 / AC7 |
| `Waiting` | `Closed` | D11 cut-off pass; D13 event cancelled |
| `Offered` | `Closed` | Expiry after the cut-off (D11); D13 event cancelled |

`Accepted`, `Withdrawn` and `Closed` are final. A rejoin creates a new entry. A place is `open` while it is offered, and `closed` once it is accepted or released to registration.

## Persistence and atomicity

- A place opening, its first offer and the D7 history row commit together with the withdrawal, or not at all.
- Releasing a hold and the next offer (or the release to registration) commit together, so there is no moment when the place is neither held nor offered (AC9).
- Accepting writes the registration and the `Accepted` entry together, re-checking `expires_at` under the event lock (no reliance on the timer having run).
- Notices are written to `waitlist_notification_outbox` in the same transaction and delivered after commit by a relay (SCRUM-64 `0037`/`0038` pattern), so a rollback sends nothing and an outage never undoes the change. Keys: `waitlist-offered:<entry>:<offered_at>`, `waitlist-expiring:<entry>:<offered_at>`, `waitlist-expired:<entry>:<offered_at>`, `waitlist-revoked:<entry>:<offered_at>`, `waitlist-closed:<entry>:<closed_at>`, `waitlist-cancelled:<entry>:<closed_at>`.
